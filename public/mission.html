Mission and Vision

Mission

To give owners of Shopify stores an independent assessment of the commercial and operational questions they place before Meridian Advisory.

Vision

To be the advisory retained by store owners who require a clear reading of sales, visibility, and the condition of the store.

About

Meridian Advisory is a private advisory practice for Shopify. Clients may request a written consultation or book a call on Zoom or Google Meet.

Principles

  • The assessment is independent of any agency already engaged by the client.
  • What a client submits is held in confidence and is not sold.
  • A reply states findings. It is not an undertaking as to sales or traffic.

Scope

  • First sales, and sales that have stopped or declined
  • Traffic, visibility, and conversion
  • Advertising, email, and retention
  • Checkout, payments, theme, applications, and speed
  • Product, pricing, orders, refunds, and chargebacks
  • Preparation for launch, and account or payout questions
