    Full name and email address are required. Country and city may be left unstated. A city can be chosen only after a country.

    The store address is required. Niche, product, year, plan, and sale dates may be left unstated. A product can be chosen only after a niche. Do not include passwords, staff invitations, or payment details.

    Choose Inquiry, Complaint, or Explanation, and the category that matches the matter. The description is required. Further detail may be added.

    Confirm that you are authorized to submit the request and that the information is accurate. The request cannot be submitted until that statement is confirmed.

    If the request is accepted, a reference number is shown. Copy it and quote it in any further correspondence.
